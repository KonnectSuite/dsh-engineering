## Principle

Colour is a signal, and about one in twelve men cannot separate red from green. Meaning must survive a
greyscale screenshot.

## Rules

- **VIEW-COLOR-01** Reserve red, amber and green for abnormal, warning and normal, and use them nowhere
  else. If a brand colour is red, do not put it on a status object.
- **VIEW-COLOR-02** Never encode meaning in colour alone: pair it with text, an icon or a shape.
- **VIEW-COLOR-03** Keep text contrast high (dark text on light surfaces) and size readable at the panel's
  viewing distance; the export's palette is the reference.

## Apply

Read the project's palette from the profile and reuse it. A new screen with a different grey is a
maintenance defect even if it is prettier.

## Anti-patterns

Rainbow trend pens; green buttons that are not "go"; white text on yellow.
