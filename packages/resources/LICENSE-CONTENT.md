# Content licence

The pages under `corpus/` are written for this bundle. They summarise engineering practice and
implementation rules; they do **not** reproduce the text of any standard or vendor manual.

Each page names its basis in front matter (`basis:`). Those names are attribution, not endorsement:

- **IEC 61131-3** — the programmable-controller languages the pages describe (ST, LD, FBD, SFC).
- **ISA-101** — human-machine interface design, especially the high-performance HMI model.
- **ISA-18.2** — alarm management lifecycle and presentation.
- **Rockwell / Yaskawa / FactoryTalk** — vendor documentation and field practice for those products.

Rule IDs (`CRAFT-NAME-01`, `VIEW-HPM-02`, …) identify this bundle's own rules. They are not the
standards' clause numbers, and a finding that cites one is this bundle's opinion about your code, not a
certification.

Standards documents are copyrighted. If you need the normative text, obtain it from the issuing body.
