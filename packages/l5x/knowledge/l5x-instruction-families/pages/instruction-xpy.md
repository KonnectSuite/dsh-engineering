XPY raises a source to a power: source X, source Y and a destination.

Revision rename: XPY is the pre-36 spelling and EXPT is the preferred spelling from revision 36. Both answer any read; the composer uses the project's preferred spelling. A mixed project is a review finding.

Offline, the operation is real-valued in general, but integer destinations truncate. Use a REAL destination and explicitly convert when the result feeds a compare with a REAL tolerance.
