Safety instructions are one family of the Logix 5000 instruction set. They are the instructions permitted in a safety task, where the tag set and placement rules are tighter.

The Studio 5000 Logix Designer v37.05 installed help indexes 40 topic pages in this family. The v36.00 help indexes 19 instruction entries in the same family. Both indexes are structural: l5x_docs lists the title, the section and the relative help path, and reads page text from the local help installation at call time. Nothing from the vendor pages ships in this repository.

Representative mnemonics from the family: Safety input/output, safety tags and safety instructions.

When editing without Studio, safety placement and safety writes are checked by l5x_run_checks; a finding must be resolved before any draft is applied to a safety-program export.

Call contents with collection "l5x-help-37-05" or "l5x-help-36-00" and pass the revision to list the family section in the installed help index, then search within it for the member you need.
