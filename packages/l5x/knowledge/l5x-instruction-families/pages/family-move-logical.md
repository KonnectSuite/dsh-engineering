Move/logical instructions are one family of the Logix 5000 instruction set. They copy or combine whole values: bitwise logical operations, moves, fills and byte swaps.

The Studio 5000 Logix Designer v37.05 installed help indexes 17 topic pages in this family. The v36.00 help indexes 15 instruction entries in the same family. Both indexes are structural: l5x_docs lists the title, the section and the relative help path, and reads page text from the local help installation at call time. Nothing from the vendor pages ships in this repository.

Representative mnemonics from the family: MOV, COP, FLL, CLR, AND, OR, XOR, NOT, SWPB.

When editing without Studio, MOV changed spelling to MOVE at revision 36; either spelling answers in the catalogue, but a mixed project is a review finding.

Call contents with collection "l5x-help-37-05" or "l5x-help-36-00" and pass the revision to list the family section in the installed help index, then search within it for the member you need.
