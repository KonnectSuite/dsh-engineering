Equipment sequence diagram instructions are one family of the Logix 5000 instruction set. They document the equipment sequence diagram elements and the instructions that interact with a running sequence.

The Studio 5000 Logix Designer v37.05 installed help indexes 22 topic pages in this family. The v36.00 help indexes 6 instruction entries in the same family. Both indexes are structural: l5x_docs lists the title, the section and the relative help path, and reads page text from the local help installation at call time. Nothing from the vendor pages ships in this repository.

Representative mnemonics from the family: SFC steps and transitions.

When editing without Studio, sequence logic is edited in its own editor; from ladder you change the sequence tag or command rather than the diagram.

Call contents with collection "l5x-help-37-05" or "l5x-help-36-00" and pass the revision to list the family section in the installed help index, then search within it for the member you need.
