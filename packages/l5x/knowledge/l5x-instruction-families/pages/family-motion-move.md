Motion move instructions are one family of the Logix 5000 instruction set. They command one motion move or axis action and report completion through status bits.

The Studio 5000 Logix Designer v37.05 installed help indexes 21 topic pages in this family. The v36.00 help indexes 12 instruction entries in the same family. Both indexes are structural: l5x_docs lists the title, the section and the relative help path, and reads page text from the local help installation at call time. Nothing from the vendor pages ships in this repository.

Representative mnemonics from the family: MAM, MAJ, MAS, MSO, MSF, MDO, MDR.

When editing without Studio, the move instruction must be scanned while its enable bit is true; confirm the axis state machine before inserting a move.

Call contents with collection "l5x-help-37-05" or "l5x-help-36-00" and pass the revision to list the family section in the installed help index, then search within it for the member you need.
